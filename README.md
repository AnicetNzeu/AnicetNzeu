### Public work

**[credit-risk-modeling](https://github.com/AnicetNzeu/credit-risk-modeling)**

A retail credit risk pipeline built end to end on 887 440 matured consumer
loans issued between 2007 and 2015: probability of default, loss given
default, exposure at default, the expected credit loss that follows from the
three, and the scorecard a credit officer would apply. Validated out of sample
and out of time, with the model's limitations written down rather than left
out. Python, statsmodels, scikit-learn.
